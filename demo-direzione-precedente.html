The dirty secret of "artisan" gelato

Here's what they hope
you never ask.

Walk into almost any gelateria in London. Look past the marble counter and the Italian flag. Then ask one simple question:

"Can I see the ingredient list for your strawberry gelato?"

Watch what happens. Because in most cases, that "strawberry" gelato never met a strawberry. It met a flavour paste. A powdered base shipped in from an industrial facility. Glucose syrup. Stabiliser E410. Emulsifier E471. Colouring E120 — which, you might want to sit down for this, is crushed insects.

They'll say "finest ingredients". They'll say "seasonal products". Notice what they don't say. They don't say fruit. Because legally, they can't.

"Others say fresh ingredients. We say FRUIT — and we put it in writing, on every label, with the name of the farm it came from."

We started Gelatorchard for one reason: to make the gelato that the marble counters only pretend to make. And we made it impossible to fake — because our entire recipe fits in six words:

Fresh fruit. Cane sugar. Water.

Why I refuse to own a single

Marco Castellano

Dear London,

My name is Marco Castellano. I'm a gelatiere, trained the Italian way — which means I was taught that gelato begins with an ingredient, not a catalogue.

When I arrived in this city, I did what any gelato maker does: I tasted everything. And I kept noticing the same strange thing. The strawberry gelato in Soho tasted identical to the strawberry gelato in Camden. Identical to the one in Notting Hill. How? Different shops, different "artisans", same taste?

Then I saw the delivery vans. The same industrial supplier, unloading the same buckets of paste and powder, at the back doors of half the "artisan" gelaterias in London. That's the day I understood: they don't make gelato. They dissolve it.

So I made myself one promise, and I built this entire company on it: if a fruit isn't in season, we don't sell that flavour. No powder has ever entered my lab. No puree. No "natural flavouring". Our supplier contracts legally forbid them — I put it in writing so I could never be tempted to cheat, even in the depths of February.

That promise costs me money every single winter. It's also the only reason my gelato tastes the way it does.

Come and watch us make it. The lab is open, the fruit is on the counter, and the label is the whole story.

Marco Castellano

Founder & Head Gelatiere, Gelatorchard

Every objection, answered straight

Questions people ask us
(and the honest answers)

"Just fruit, sugar and water" — really? What's the catch?

No catch. Every fruit flavour: fresh fruit (60–70% of what's in your cup), unrefined cane sugar, water. Our supplier contracts legally exclude powders, pastes and flavourings — it's not a policy, it's a signed obligation. Milk-based classics add fresh whole milk, and that's it.

Why does it cost more than supermarket gelato?

Because a kilo of fresh Kent strawberries costs roughly ten times what a scoop of strawberry flavour powder costs. You're not paying for our brand. You're paying for the actual fruit — 60–70% of every cup. Powder gelato isn't cheaper because it's clever. It's cheaper because there's almost nothing in it.

Is it vegan? What about allergies?

All fruit sorbets are naturally vegan and free from the 14 major allergens — no dairy, gluten, eggs or nuts, because fruit doesn't contain them. Nothing was "reformulated" to achieve this. Milk-based flavours are clearly marked.

Why is my favourite flavour gone?

Because its season ended. We physically cannot make Kent strawberry gelato in December without powder — and we will never use powder. Your flavour returns when the fruit does. That annual wait is precisely why it tastes the way it does.

What do you do in winter, then?

Winter has its own fruit: IGP Sicilian citrus, quince, pomegranate — plus a small disclosed selection of high-quality frozen single-origin fruit, with origin printed on the menu. Disclosed means disclosed: if it was frozen, the label says so.

Do you deliver?

Yes — across London via Deliveroo and UberEats in insulated packaging, plus the Gelato Lab Kit to assemble at home. Use code REALFRUIT for £3 off your first order.
